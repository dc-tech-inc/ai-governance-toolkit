# Alignment map (topic level, own words)

ISO/IEC 42005:2025 (AI system impact assessment) is copyrighted. This file does **not** quote it, does not reproduce its clause titles or numbers, and was written from public descriptions of what the standard is about, not from the licensed text. Treat it as a reading aid for this tool, not as a statement of what the standard requires. Buy the standard on the [official ISO page](https://www.iso.org/standard/42005.html) and check this map against it.

What the public descriptions say the standard is: a first edition, published in 2025 by ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 as **guidance** (not a certifiable requirements standard) on how and when to assess the impact of an AI system on people and society, what to document, and how that process fits into an organisation's wider risk and management processes. It sits next to ISO/IEC 42001, which is the management-system standard.

| This tool's section | The topic it covers | Where the standard is generally described as covering it |
|---|---|---|
| 1. System overview | What the system is, its owner, purpose, lifecycle stage, deployment context | Defining the scope and context of an assessment |
| 2. Uses and misuse | Intended uses and foreseeable misuse | Understanding how the system is meant to be, and may be, used |
| 3. Stakeholders | Who is affected, and who may need extra care | Identifying affected parties, including those who are not users |
| 4. Data and model | Technique, data sources, personal data, human oversight | Describing the system characteristics that bear on impact |
| 5. Impacts | Benefits and harms, scope, rating before and after mitigation | Analysing and evaluating impacts, and treating them |
| 6. Monitoring | Indicators, review cadence, incident contact | Keeping the assessment current over the lifecycle |
| 7. Review and sign-off | Reviewer, dates, status | Documenting and approving the assessment |

## What this tool deliberately does not do

- It does not follow the standard's template or headings. It has its own.
- It does not define harm categories, thresholds or a scoring method from the standard. The 1-5 by 1-5 scoring is a plain convention ([ADR 3](adr/0003-simple-risk-scoring.md)).
- It does not claim, and must not be described as, conformant, compliant or certified. Nothing in it is an audit.
